## TL;DR

Anthropic publicly filed its S-1 on October 26 (NASDAQ: ANT; H1 2026 revenue $16.3B; Reuters ~$1.5T valuation, down from the $2T target) — the biggest AI IPO catalyst of the month — with a roadshow targeting the week of November 9 and a listing before Thanksgiving (November 26). Watch November for Anthropic roadshow/pricing, CBRS lockup + earnings, AMD Q3 (November 3), NVDA Q3 (est. November 18), and SpaceX's December 8/9 full block expiry.
